ABOUT MA'DIN SUFFA CAMPUS

Where Qur’anic Excellence Meets Modern Education

domain_verification Affiliated with Jamiathul Hind Al Islamiyya — Centre ID: INS7572

Ma'din Suffa Campus, Vavoor is an exclusive educational institution for Huffazul Qur'an, functioning under Ma'din Academy and affiliated with Jamiathul Hind Al Islamiyya. Located in Vavoor, Malappuram District, Kerala, the campus combines intensive Qur'anic memorization revision with advanced Islamic education, Da'wa studies, contemporary knowledge, and university collegiate streams such as Commerce and Humanities. The objective is to develop knowledgeable scholars and future leaders who combine traditional Islamic values with modern education and contemporary awareness.

Campus Academic Prospectus

Ma'din Suffa Campus Vavoor (Affiliated: INS7572)

Academic Overview

Ma'din Suffa Campus, Vavoor offers an integrated academic environment under Ma'din Academy and Jamiathul Hind Al Islamiyya (Centre Code: INS7572). Designed exclusively for Huffazul Qur'an, the campus delivers dual-track collegiate education with high scholastic rigor.

Integrated Syllabi
  • Uloomul Qur’an & Tajweed Mastery
  • Tafseer & Classical Hadith Sciences
  • Da'wa Studies & Contemporary Discourse
  • University Bachelor Degree (Commerce / Arts)
Advanced Mentorship
  • UGC NET & JRF Research Foundation
  • Multilingual Eloquence (English, Arabic, Malayalam)
  • Literary Arts, Debate & Calligraphy
  • Leadership and Community Engagement
Admission Protocol & Eligibility

• Eligibility: Only certified Huffaz who completed Holy Qur'an memorization with recognized secondary academic credentials.
• Selection Mode: Written aptitude, Tajweed oral evaluation, and academic interview.
• Hostel & Campus Life: Complete residential boarding with state-of-the-art scholastic Halaqa halls and academic coaching facilities.
